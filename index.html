Meet Squishy

Say hello to Squishy — the handsome black cat behind the name of our bakery! He’s our beloved house cat and the true heart of Squishy Cakes Bakery. While he prefers to stay cozy indoors, he’s very much the inspiration behind every batch of baked goods we make.


He’s jet black, wildly energetic, and one of the friendliest cats you’ll ever meet — always zooming around the house and making sure everyone knows he’s in charge. He can’t help us cook, but he’s an absolute pro at making biscuits. 🐾 We bake with love, and Squishy is what that love looks like. 💜
